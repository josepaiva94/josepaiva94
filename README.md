## 🚀 About Me

I am a **Solutions Architect**, **AI Specialist**, and **PhD Computer Scientist** with **10+ years of experience** architecting high-scalability web platforms, AI-driven educational environments, multi-tenant SaaS platforms, and automated assessment systems.

- 🎓 **PhD Research**: Completed my Doctorate at the **Faculty of Sciences, University of Porto** on _"Improving Feedback in the Automated Assessment of Programming Assignments Using Students' Past Solutions"_. Author of the landmark **ACM TOCE state-of-the-art review** on automated assessment.
- 🤖 **AI & RAG Innovation**: Architecting production LLM pipelines, vector databases (Qdrant), pedagogical AI agents (**GamiBot**), and semantic code representation engines.
- 🏢 **Enterprise & EdTech Systems**: Designed and deployed enterprise portals (**HUB Digital**, **Academ_IA XP**, **CIP Web Portal**) serving higher education consortia and thousands of learners.
- 💼 **Consultancy**: CEO of **Kriol.IO** and **Toptal Vetted Engineer**, delivering end-to-end cloud applications, custom APIs, and system integrations globally.
